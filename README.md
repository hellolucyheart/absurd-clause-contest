One absurd clause hidden deep inside a terms-of-service agreement — a single original English sentence of at most 200 characters, written as deadpan legal boilerplate: a numbered section, a wall of licensing adjectives, and the quiet reveal that what you are licensing away is your soul, where applicable, including any afterlife rights.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It validates the clause (one line, one sentence, 1–200 characters) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
